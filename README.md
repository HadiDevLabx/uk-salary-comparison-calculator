# Salary Comparison Calculator UK — Compare Two Job Offers Properly

> A £45,000 offer with a 3% pension is worth less than a £43,000 offer with 8%, once employer contributions are counted.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Comparing headline salaries alone is the most common mistake in a job move. What matters is take-home **plus** money into your pension.

| | Offer A | Offer B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Salary | £45,000 | £43,000 |
| Take-home | £35,919.60 | £34,479.60 |
| Employer pension at 3% / 8% | £1,350 | £3,440 |
| **Total value** | **£37,269.60** | **£37,919.60** |

The lower salary wins by £650.00 a year, and that is before compounding.

## What this does not cover

It cannot price the things that decide most moves: commute, hours, security, and whether you will still want the job in a year.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
